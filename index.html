Who We Are

Rooted in Community, Growing with Purpose

Youth Service, Nepali Soul

Leo Club is the youth organization of Lions Clubs International, empowering young people to serve their communities and develop leadership skills. We are Leo Club of Kathmandu Laligurans — proudly serving under Leo District Council 325, Nepal.

The name "Laligurans" (लालीगुराँस) means rhododendron — Nepal's national flower. Just like this resilient bloom that paints our hills crimson every spring, our club represents the vibrant spirit of Nepali youth.

Based in Naikap, West Kathmandu, we are deeply rooted in our local community while reaching for horizons beyond. From heritage trails to environmental action, we believe every act of service counts.

"We Serve" — not just a motto, but a way of life for every Leo.

Our Impact

What We Do

Heritage & Hiking Trips

Exploring trails like Indrathan, Ghatte Danda, and Switzerland Chaur in the Kathmandu Valley. We believe the best way to appreciate our heritage is to walk through it.

Creative Writing & Blogging

Members write essays, poetry in English & Nepali, and stories published on our club blog. Giving voice to youth perspectives on culture, nature, and society.

Heritage Promotion

Documenting and promoting undervisited temples like Sansari Mandir and historical Kritipur-era sites. Preserving stories that risk being forgotten.

Environment & Nature Awareness

"Save the Nature" campaigns and appreciation of Nepal's natural landscape. From tree plantations to cleanup drives — we act for our planet.

Community Service

Participating in heritage-in-youth programs alongside Lions Club of Kathmandu Laligurans. Serving our community with hands, hearts, and hope.

Youth Development

Leadership workshops, fellowship events, and skill-building under Leo District Council 325, Nepal. Building tomorrow's leaders today.
